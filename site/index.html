Method and sources

  • Storage data: GIE AGSI+ (Gas Infrastructure Europe), country and EU totals through the AGSI+ API, refreshed daily. Each gas day shows the stock at the end of that day. AGSI+ publishes at 19:30 CET with a second run at 23:00 for late reporters; each update re-reads the last 60 days, because operators correct recent days. Days marked e are still estimated by the operator (status E), the rest are confirmed (C). Field definitions: AGSI+ data definitions.
  • Fill %: gas in storage as a share of working gas volume (AGSI+ “full”). TWh is gas in storage; capacity is the working gas volume.
  • 5-year average and range: the same calendar day in the five previous years (29 February uses 28 February), so 2026 is compared with 2021–2025 and a day in 2021 with 2016–2020. The range is the lowest and highest of those five. Shown only when at least three of the five years have data.
  • Net flow: injection minus withdrawal, GWh per day; positive means the country is filling. Long periods are shown as weekly or monthly averages.
  • % of annual consumption: AGSI+ “consumption full”, gas in storage against the country's reference annual consumption. ≈ days of consumption = that share × 365, i.e. days of average demand (winter demand is higher).
  • Filling target: under Regulation (EU) 2025/1733, EU countries with storage must reach 90% at any point between 1 October and 1 December, with room to deviate in difficult market conditions; the rules run until the end of 2027. The chart draws that line and window for EU countries and the EU total, not for the UK. Earlier years had a fixed date under Regulation (EU) 2022/1032: 80% by 1 November 2022, 90% by 1 November in 2023–2025.
  • TTF: TTF spot, EUR/MWh.
  • Map: borders from entsoe-py (MIT) and Natural Earth (public domain). Grey countries aren't tracked yet.
